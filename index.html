A small boat on a small planet: a low-poly water world to sail round and fish on, drawn in the browser with three.js (WebGPU and TSL shaders).

Made by Senne De Backer.

The sea is a port of the Shadertoy “3d simplex noise” by Nikita Miropolskiy (MIT), and the rain ripples and the wake come from other Shadertoy shaders. Every credit is in the README.
