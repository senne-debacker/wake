id:           w_005-riverlands
name:         The Riverlands
description:  Rolling green hills with slow rivers, reed beds and willow lakes winding between them.
price:        900
fish:         w_005-riverlands
anything special:
  - Made from blender/worlds/w_005-riverlands.blend on top of the Wake base file. Mostly land (about
    two thirds): you start in the River Mouth and sail up the Meanders; the rivers (1.0-1.6 wide) form one
    network with a loop (Meanders -> Narrows -> Reedmarsh -> Willow Lake -> back to the Meanders) and a long
    river round the far side past the Hollow.
  - Wide Water (the biggest lake), Willow Lake (willows, lily pads with white flowers), Reedmarsh (three
    reed islets with channels either side), the Narrows (a rocky gorge under a rope bridge, 0.78 clearance)
    and the old Stone Bridge (arch springs at 0.74).
  - Bram the ropemaker's mill on the millpond, with the ropewalk beside it. His dock is the selling point
    (Dock_Bram) and is solid (the Bram_Dock group). The mill wheel (Mill_Wheel, pivot on its axle) is jammed
    (Mill_Jam): the game does not turn it yet.
  - Markers the game does not read yet: Area_*, Quest_Logjam, Quest_WheelPaddle_1-3 (the paddles float
    as WheelPaddle_1-3), HousePlot and Resident_Bram.
  - It has its own 32 fish (fish: w_005-riverlands): river fish, with the times of day and the weather of the design brief.
    Map areas, rod groups and the lamp / mill wheel conditions are not mechanics of the game yet.
