anything special:
  - Made from blender/worlds/w_002-frostmere.blend. Tall white ridges
    (peaks up to 2.0) form the Long Fjord, a winding channel about 1.3 wide that ends at Ezra's
    Inlet, where Ezra the cartographer lives in a hut on stilts. A wider way leaves the inlet
    to the north-east.
  - Ezra's dock is the selling point (Dock_Ezra) and is solid (the Ezra_Dock group).
  - Waterfall Cove, under the tallest cliffs, has a sea cave you can sail into (the cave roof is
    a prop, Cave_Roof).
  - A blocky glacier with broken ice chunks, the Icefield of drifting floes and small bergs, the
    Black Skerries, five low pine holms and three outer crags near the fjord; on the far side a ring of
    eight snowy islands (most with pines) round the Cold Deep, the open water at the south pole.
  - Seven orange survey flags, and markers the game does not read yet: Area_*, Quest_CompassPiece_1-4
    and Resident_Ezra.
  - Suggested mood if the game ever reads one: colder sea colour, snow instead of rain, a pale low sun,
    the aurora at night.
